Life & Death Map

How the clues work

Two places and two years describe a life

Each round shows the year and location of a person's birth and death. The map is the main clue: sometimes the two places are close together, while other lives stretch across countries or continents. You identify the person from those combined signals.

1. Read the map

Use the birth and death markers to think about where the person lived and which historical setting connects the two locations.

2. Use the dates

The two years help narrow the period. If you need more help, an occupation hint can reveal what the person is known for.

3. Keep trying

An incorrect guess does not end the round. You can try another name or choose “I don’t know” and reveal the answer.

What it trains

Historical geography

The game is designed to reinforce the relationship between biography and geography. Birthplaces can suggest language, nationality or political context, while places of death often reveal migration, exile, travel or the international reach of a person's career.

You can leave place names hidden for a harder map-reading challenge or show them before guessing when you want to focus on historical knowledge rather than geography.
