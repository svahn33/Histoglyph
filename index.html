Game modes

More than a quiz

Learn history by connecting people, places, time and faces

One shared historical database powers three different challenges. The goal is not just to recognise a name, but to connect each person to where and when they lived — and to recognise the images that made them familiar.

Geography

See where a life unfolded

Life & Death Map turns birthplace and place of death into visual clues. It encourages you to associate historical figures with the geography of their lives instead of learning dates in isolation.

Explore Life & Death Map →

Chronology

Build a timeline from memory

Timeline asks you to place people in chronological order. Every correct placement becomes a new reference point, so the challenge gradually builds a mental picture of which lives overlapped.

Explore Timeline →

Recognition

Put faces to historical names

Portrait begins with the image alone. Optional clues can reveal life years, occupations or places, allowing you to decide how much information you need before making a guess.

Explore Portrait →

Collections & filters

Play broadly or narrow the question

Collections define which historical figures can appear. World History mixes people from different regions and periods, while American Presidents, World War I and World War II let you test a more focused field of knowledge.

Before a game starts, you can refine the selection by difficulty, birth-year range and game length. Life & Death Map also offers timed play and hidden place names, while Portrait lets you decide which clues are visible from the start.
