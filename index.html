Game modes

More than a quiz

Learn history by connecting people, places and time

Histoglyph is built around one historical dataset that is reused across several kinds of challenges. The goal is not just to recognise a name, but to connect that person to a place, a period and the people who lived before or after them.

Geography

See where a life unfolded

Life & Death Map turns birthplace and place of death into visual clues. It encourages you to associate historical figures with the geography of their lives instead of learning dates in isolation.

Explore Life & Death Map →

Chronology

Build a timeline from memory

Timeline asks you to place people in chronological order. Every correct placement becomes a new reference point, so the challenge gradually builds a mental picture of which lives overlapped.

Explore Timeline →

Recognition

Put faces to historical names

Portrait begins with the image alone. Optional clues can reveal life years, occupations or places, allowing you to decide how much information you need before making a guess.

Explore Portrait →

Collections & filters

Play broadly or narrow the question

Collections define which historical figures can appear. World History mixes people from different regions and periods, while focused collections such as American Presidents let you test a narrower field of knowledge.

Before a game starts, you can refine the selection by difficulty and birth-year range. You can play a fixed number of rounds or include every person that matches your filters.
